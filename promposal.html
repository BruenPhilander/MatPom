A little something I wrote for you ♡

As the lights dim with a golden glow,
the sun gracefully sets upon us,
my heart, embarrassed and scared.
It’s not the music that troubles it,
but the scenery of her.

As my heart reaches out its hand,
it trembles.
As my heart speaks,
its voice plays out of tune.

It asks…

Can I have your hand,
as we dance away into the cold night,
creating many more happy memories
to look back on?

Will you be my forever dance partner?

Not just for the Matric Ball,
but for the rest of my long life.

Will you accompany me
as we tango,
as we waltz,
or as we Just Dance?

I love you more than anything,
and I want to learn my first slow dance
with my most favourite person in the world.

And after all these memories...

Will you make
one more with me? ♡

Will you go to Matric Dance with me?
